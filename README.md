### A simple way to submit eHours
HourSync is a modern app to replace the Olathe School District's [Academy Endorsement Portal](https://academyendorsement.olatheschools.com).
Their website is inconvenient, outdated, and not user-friendly. HourSync is a modern, concise, and user-friendly way to submit eHours.

# Features
- **Modern Design**: HourSync is designed to be modern and user-friendly. It's built on the WinUI 3 framework, so you can blame Microsoft if the apps overall design isn't to your standards
- **Compatible with Accesibility Themes**: HourSync is compatible with Windows' accessibility themes, so it's actually usable if you have impaired vision or need a contrast theme to differentiate items.
- **Drafts**: We all know the pain of submitting a lengthy eHours request, only to have it lost since you were signed out. HourSync saves your progress as you go, so you can come back to it later.
- **Automatic Re-login**: HourSync will automatically sign you back in if you're signed out, so you don't have to worry about losing your progress.
- **ChatGPT Sidebar**: HourSync has a built-in ChatGPT sidebar when submitting eHour requests, so it becomes a breeze to write a description of your eHours.
- **Improved Image Viewer**: HourSync has an improved image viewer, so you can see your uploaded images in a new window, and your images are not stretched to oblivion.
- **Browser View**: Pop out the AI companion if you're running low on horizontal screen real-estate. You can also view eHour requests in the browser in case something goes wrong.

# Installation
Download the latest release of HourSync from the releases tab, or by visiting [this link](https://hoursync.net/download).

# Usage
Sign in with your laptop login credentials, and you'll be presented with a list of past and pending requests. No more needing to click the "eHours" tab for no reason!

Submitting eHours is a breeze. Click the "Create Submission" button on the left, and start writing. Enjoy the ChatGPT sidebar to help you write a description of your eHours. If you need to step away, you no longer have to worry about an issue with your laptop ruining your hard work.
HourSync will save your progress as you go, so you can come back to it later.
